# Gray Sisterhood Module
Theme pack of content revolving around the Gray Maidens. Adds an *Aid Another* action and various perks that increase its effectiveness.

## Actions
- **Aid Another**<sup>[VN](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/gamemastering/Combat/#TOC-Aid-Another)</sup>

## Archetypes
### **Barbarian:**


- **Dreadnought**<sup>[VN](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/core-classes/barbarian/archetypes/paizo-barbarian-archetypes/dreadnought-barbarian-archetype/)</sup>
    - **Dead Calm** - Alters Rage. Gain half of the usual barbarian rage bonuses, plus an additional bonus to Intimidation. The Barbarian is not exhausted after using Dead Calm but cannot enter a  new rage for 1 minute.
    - **Steady Gait** - Replaces Fast Movement. Gain a bonus to saving throws against movement-impairing, ground-based, and trap spells or effects.
    - **Dread Pressence** - Replaces Greater Rage. Bonuses from Dead Calm increase by 1 (to a total of +2).
    - **Fearless Killer** - Replaces Indomitable Will. Gain Fearless Killer as a bonus feat.
    - **Instant Dispassion** - Replaces Tireless Rage. The Barbarian can enter a new rage at any time after the previous rage ends.
    - **Pursuer** - Replaces Mighty Rage. Bonuses from Dead Calm increase by 1 (to a total of +3).
    - **Extra Dead Calm** - Can be selected as Basic Feat. Gain 6 additional rounds of Dead Calm per day.

### **Cavalier:**
- **Sister-In-Arms**<sup>[VN](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/base-classes/cavalier/archetypes/paizo-cavalier-archetypes/sister-in-arms-cavalier-archetype/)</sup>
    - **Halfhearted Challenge** - Alters Challenge. Add only half of the cavalier level to damage rolls against the challenged target.
    - **Maiden's Order** - Alters Order and replaces Mount. Instead of selecting an order, the cavalier treated as a member of both the Order of the Lion and the Order of the Dragon at the same time.
    - **Devoted Defender** - Replaces Cavalier's Charge. Gain Bodyguard as a bonus feat.
    - **Maiden's Loyalty** - Gain a bonus to saving throws against charm, compulsion, and confusion effects.
    - **Dedicated Commander** - Replaces Mighty Charge. The cavalier can use Lion's Call and Strategy as a move action.
    - **Supreme Commander** - Replaces Supreme Charge. The cavalier can use Lion's Call, Strategy, and Act as One as a swift action.
