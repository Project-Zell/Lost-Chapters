# Gray Sisterhood Module
Theme pack of content revolving around the Gray Maidens. Adds an *Aid Another* action and various perks that increase its effectiveness.

## Archetypes
### **Barbarian:**


- **Dreadnought**<sup>[VN](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/core-classes/barbarian/archetypes/paizo-barbarian-archetypes/dreadnought-barbarian-archetype/)</sup>
    - **Dead Calm** - Alters Rage. Gain half of the usual Barbarian Rage bonuses, plus an additional bonus to Intimidation. The Barbarian is not exhausted after using Dead Calm but cannot enter a  new rage for 1 minute.
    - **Steady Gait** - Replaces Fast Movement. Gain a bonus (which increases every 5 levels) to saving throws against movement-impairing, ground-based, and trap spells or effects.
    - **Dread Pressence** - Replaces Greater Rage. Bonuses from Dead Calm increase by 1 (to a total of +2).
    - **Fearless Killer** - Replaces Indomitable Will. Gain Fearless Killer as a bonus feat.
    - **Instant Dispassion** - Replaces Tireless Rage. The Barbarian can enter a new rage at any time after the previous rage ends.
    - **Pursuer** - Replaces Mighty Rage. Bonuses from Dead Calm increase by 1 (to a total of +3).
    - **Extra Dead Calm** - Can be selected as Basic Feat. Gain 6 additional rounds of Dead Calm per day.

### **Cavalier:**
- **Sister-In-Arms**<sup>[VN](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/base-classes/cavalier/archetypes/paizo-cavalier-archetypes/sister-in-arms-cavalier-archetype/)</sup>
    - **Halfhearted Challenge** - Alters Challenge. Add only half of the Cavalier level to damage rolls against the challenged target.
    - **MaidenÅfs Order** - Alters Order and replaces Mount. Instead of selecting an order, the Cavalier counts as a member of both the Order of the Lion and the Order of the Dragon at the same time.
    - **Devoted Defender** - Replaces CavalierÅfs Charge. Gain Bodyguard as a bonus feat.
    - **MaidenÅfs Loyalty** - Gain a bonus (which increases every 4 levels) to saving throws against charm, compulsion, and confusion effects.
    - **Dedicated Commander** - Replaces Mighty Charge. The Cavalier can use LionÅfs Call and Strategy as a move action.
    - **Supreme Commander** - Replaces Supreme Charge. The Cavalier can use LionÅfs Call, Strategy, and Act as One as a swift action.

<summary>Blessed Incense Selection</summary>

#### Air Domain
- **Sparkling Fume** - An affected ally deals an additional 1d6 points of electricity damage with each weapon strike.
- **Sparkling Fume, Fortified** - An affected ally deals an additional 2d6 points of electricity damage with every weapon strike. In addition, the ally gains electricity resistance equal to 10 + the deacon's level.
#### Animal Domain
- **Savage Breath** - The first time each round that an affected ally hits an enemy, the enemy begins to bleed, taking 1d6 damage each round.
- **Savage Breath, Fortified** - The first time each round that an affected ally hits an enemy, the enemy begins to bleed, taking 2d6 damage each round. In addition, the ally gains the pounce special ability, allowing them to make a full attack at the end of a charge.
#### Artifice Domain
- **Pulverized Dust** - An affected ally gains damage reduction DR 2/-.
- **Pulverized Dust, Fortified** - An affected ally gains damage reduction DR 5/-.
#### Chaos Domain
- **Chaos Embrace** - Any lawful enemy creature who enters the area of the blessed incense must succeed on a Fortitude save or become alchemically sickened for 1d4 rounds. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half the deacon's level + the deacon's Wisdom modifier.
- **Chaos Embrace, Fortified** - Any lawful enemy creature who enters the area of the blessed incense must succeed on a Fortitude save or become both alchemically nauseated and alchemically sickened for 1d4 rounds. If the save succeeds, the creature is only alchemically sickened for 1d4 rounds. The DC of this save is equal to 10 + half the deacon's level + the deacon's Wisdom modifier.
#### Charm Domain
- **Alluring Mist** - An affected ally receives a +2 alchemical bonus to Charisma. An affected enemy suffers a -2 alchemical penalty to mind-affecting effects.
- **Alluring Mist, Fortified** - An affected ally receives a +4 alchemical bonus to Charisma. An affected enemy suffers a -4 alchemical penalty to mind-affecting effects.
#### Community Domain
- **Focusing Fog** - An affected ally receives a +2 alchemical bonus to attacks performed with ranged weapons. The deacon receives an additional +1 bonus.
- **Focusing Fog, Fortified** - An affected ally receives a +4 alchemical bonus to attacks performed with ranged weapons. The deacon receives an additional +2 bonus.
#### Darkness Domain
- **Gloomveil** - An affected ally receives concealment (15% miss chance).
- **Gloomveil, Fortified** - An affected ally receives concealment (30% miss chance).
#### Death Domain
- **Perdition Haze** - An affected ally deals an additional 1d6 points of negative damage with each weapon strike.
- **Perdition Haze, Fortified** - An affected ally deals an additional 2d6 points of negative damage with every weapon strike. In addition, the ally gains positive resistance equal to 10 + the deacon's level.
